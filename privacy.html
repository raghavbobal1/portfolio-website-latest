Visitor analytics

If I activate Google Analytics 4, you will be asked before any Google Analytics tracking script loads. If you allow analytics, it can collect page views, referral sources, general device/browser information and approximate country or city based on technical signals such as network location. Google Analytics may use cookies or other identifiers. Reports are aggregated and may use privacy thresholds.

Analytics does not tell me a visitor's name, employer, email address or precise street address. I do not deliberately send names, form entries, email addresses or other personal details as analytics events.

Your choice

Analytics is optional. Declining will not block any portfolio content, project case study or game. Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the website remembers it. You can change it at any time below; if you withdraw consent after accepting, the site reloads to stop additional analytics requests. Previous data already collected by Google Analytics is not automatically erased by withdrawing consent.
